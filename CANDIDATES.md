# Candidate lines — The One-Line Laugh

Judged against the house brief, most important first: (1) funny and within
200 characters as one sentence; (2) a real surprise at the end that still
makes sense; (3) an everyday truth people recognise; (4) economy of words;
(5) freshness.

## 1. CHOSEN — 99 chars
> My smartwatch congratulated me for standing up, which is the saddest applause I have ever received.
>
> Verdict: strongest. The twist ("saddest applause") lands last and
> reframes the whole line; praising trivial movement is a universal
> fitness-tracker truth; 99 chars with no wasted word; the phrasing is
> original, not a recycled joke format.

## 2. — 90 chars
> My car's check-engine light has been on so long I've started treating it as a night-light.
>
> Verdict: runner-up. Clean surprise reframe and the most economical, but
> check-engine-light jokes are well-trodden, so it scores lower on freshness
> than the smartwatch line.

## 3. — 108 chars
> I bought noise-cancelling headphones to hear myself think, and all I heard was my stomach asking for snacks.
>
> Verdict: good surprise and a relatable truth, but the setup runs longer
> than the payoff and the hunger punchline is a familiar move.

## 4. — 119 chars
> My password needs a capital, a number, and a symbol, which is more commitment than my last relationship ever asked for.
>
> Verdict: the relationship comparison is a real twist, but password jokes
> are among the most told one-liners around, so freshness is its weak point.

## 5. — 101 chars
> I set five alarms to wake up early, and now I sleep through all five in perfect harmony like a choir.
>
> Verdict: charming image and a true everyday habit, but "like a choir" is
> a gentle smile rather than a laugh — the weakest punchline of the five.

## 6. — 122 chars
> I told my plants I loved them every day, and they still died, which is how I learned affection is not a watering schedule.
>
> Verdict: a neat final turn and a real houseplant truth, but the longest
> candidate and the setup outweighs the payoff.
