One funny line, written to stand alone as a single sentence: a smartwatch
congratulates its wearer for standing up, and the wearer hears the saddest
applause of their life. The joke turns on a small everyday truth — fitness
trackers praise the tiniest movement — and lands its surprise at the very
end, in 99 characters with nothing wasted. Five candidate lines were drafted
and scored against the judge's brief (surprise ending, everyday truth,
economy, freshness); the strongest is the one this program writes, and the
full shortlist with one-line verdicts is in `CANDIDATES.md`.

## Run it

Requires Python 3 (standard library only, no dependencies):

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line against the competition gate — one sentence, 1 to 200
characters, not only whitespace — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`
(or to the path in `ENTRY_OUTPUT`).
